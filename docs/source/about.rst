****************
About FAIRLinked
****************

FAIRLinked supports the FAIRification of materials science data: making data
more findable, accessible, interoperable, and reusable. It aligns tabular and
semi-structured datasets with MDS-Onto and converts them into Linked Data
representations that preserve scientific context and relationships.

The package is actively developed and maintained by the **SDLE Research Center
at Case Western Reserve University**.
